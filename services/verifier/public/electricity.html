Support

Questions, answered

How does signing in with my digital ID work?

Choose “Sign in”, scan the QR code with your wallet (or show your wallet’s QR to the camera in person) and approve. We receive only your name, ID number, date of birth and an “over 18” proof.

Is my identity verified?

Yes. Your credential is signed by a trusted issuer and bound to your phone, so it cannot be copied or reused by someone else.

What if I do not have a digital ID yet?

Get one in a few minutes from the identity issuer with your passport or ID card, then come back and sign in.
